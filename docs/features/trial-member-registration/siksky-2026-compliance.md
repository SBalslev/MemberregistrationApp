# Trial member compliance for SIKSKY 2026

**Scope:** Club policy and check-in flow aligned with SIKSKY 2026 (April 2026).
**Applies to:** Trial shooting and member check-in for registreringspligtige and non-registreringspligtige vaaben.

## Key definitions

- **Trial shooter (registreringspligtige):** Any person without approved SKV2 or SKV6 who wants to shoot registreringspligtige vaaben. This is a legal definition and does not depend on membership payment status.
- **Proevemedlem:** Internal club status for a person who has registered but not yet paid kontingent. This is a club policy term.
- **Non-registreringspligtige vaaben:** Air 4.5 mm in our club.

## Policy summary

- Trial shooting with registreringspligtige vaaben is limited to **proevemedlemmer** who have **not paid kontingent**.
- Anyone **without SKV2 or SKV6** is still a **trial shooter** for registreringspligtige vaaben, even if they have paid kontingent.
- Members over 15 must have **approved SKV2 or SKV6** before they can shoot registreringspligtige vaaben.
- Trial shooting with air 4.5 mm does **not** require ID check or digital logbook by law, but the club may still choose to apply the same workflow for consistency.

## Trial shooting rules for registreringspligtige vaaben

- Max 3 trial sessions per person within 12 months
- Original photo ID required for all ages
- Digital logbook required with:
  - Name
  - ID type
  - ID number
  - Date of each session
- Logbook must be retained for 5 years and produced to police on request
- Trial shooting must be supervised by instructor or trainer
- 4th session requires:
  - Paid membership
  - Approved SKV2 or SKV6 for anyone over 15

## Trial shooting rules for air 4.5 mm

- No legal requirement for ID check
- No legal requirement for digital logbook
- No legal limit on number of trial sessions

## Check-in flow

### 1. Identify weapon type

- If the session uses registreringspligtige vaaben, follow the registreringspligtige flow
- If the session uses air 4.5 mm, follow the air flow

### 2. Registreringspligtige flow

- If member has approved SKV2 or SKV6:
  - Allow check-in
  - No trial logbook
- If member does not have SKV2 or SKV6:
  - Only allow if they are a proevemedlem and have not paid kontingent
  - Verify original photo ID
  - Log trial session in digital logbook
  - Ensure this is within the 3 in 12 months limit

### 3. Air 4.5 mm flow

- If proevemedlem or member:
  - Allow check-in
  - No legal ID or logbook required

## Notes for admins

- SKV3 is required only for access to weapon storage and transport permission, not for shooting registreringspligtige vaaben.
- A member who has paid kontingent but lacks SKV2 or SKV6 cannot shoot registreringspligtige vaaben.
- Update privacy policy to state that trial shooter data is collected and retained for 5 years.
